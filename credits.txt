Laddoo Chor: a YouTube Playable from the cartoon series "Natkhat Chhora vs the Cockroach Gang"
(the YouTube channel "Everything is Here").

Characters (the pagdi boy, the Cockroach Gang, Mota Chuha the rat), drawings, game design, music and sound effects are
original and were made in code for the series and this game. The music is the series' own theme, synthesised in the
browser with the Web Audio API. There are no audio files, images or third-party code in this game.
