Laddoo Chor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: Laddoo Chor collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

Laddoo Chor is a free tap game made by the creator of the YouTube channel Everything is Here, from the cartoon series Natkhat Chhora vs the Cockroach Gang. This policy covers the Laddoo Chor Android app and the web version of the game.

What the game stores

  • Your best score and number of games played are saved only on your own device, so the game can show your best score next time. They never leave the device, and uninstalling the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes them.
  • When the game is played inside YouTube as a YouTube Playable, YouTube may keep that same small save (your best score) with your YouTube account. That is handled by YouTube under Google's Privacy Policy.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It does not use the camera, microphone or location, and the Android app asks for no permissions.
  • The Android app works fully offline: all the game's pictures and sounds are inside the app, and it does not connect to the internet.
  • It shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, no chat, and no links to other websites or apps.
  • It does not share or sell any data, because it has none to share.

Children

Laddoo Chor is a cartoon game for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
